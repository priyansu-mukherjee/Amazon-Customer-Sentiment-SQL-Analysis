# E-Commerce Customer Experience & Operational Analytics (SQL)

## Overview
Analyzed 20K+ Amazon customer reviews using SQL to identify sentiment patterns, customer satisfaction trends, and operational bottlenecks.

## Key Findings
* **Sentiment Breakdown:** Categorized reviews to reveal that feedback was heavily negative.
* **Rating Trends:** Identified a severe multi-year drop from historical peak ratings to low recent averages.
* **Root-Cause Analysis:** Proved that customer support and delivery delays drive most negative ratings rather than product defects.
* **Market Segmentation:** Showed that customer review volume is overwhelmingly dominated by the US and UK markets.

## How to Run
Open `Analysis_On_Amazon_reviews_dataset` in MySQL Workbench or any standard SQL client and execute against the reviews dataset.
